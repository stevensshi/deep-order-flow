# Data notice

This repository contains no market data: no ITCH or LOBSTER file, no order-book table, and no price, size, order or
message.

- Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH 5.0 files and LOBSTER files are obtained from their providers under their own terms.
  The code reads them from paths given on the command line or in `DOF_*` environment variables.
- `results/` holds statistics computed from licensed data: per stock, day and horizon the out-of-sample R² with its
  sums of squared errors and of squares (the earlier stage's ridge rows keep R² only); per held-out stock-day the
  ratio of its median mid price to that of a reference day, and the stock-days left out with their reason; per fit
  the validation losses and the sha256 of the saved weights. The weights themselves are not included.
- `configs/universe_115.csv` lists the 115 tickers of the universe. `configs/protocol/corporate_actions_smoke_fixture.csv`
  is a test file of invented events.
- `configs/protocol/corporate_actions_20251210_20260612.csv`: four corporate actions with quotes from public SEC filings.
- The committed golden files hold hashes and score statistics only: hashes of arrays and files, floats written
  as `float.hex`, counts, and sums of squared errors. Only goldens computed from seeded synthetic data are
  committed; goldens computed from licensed data stay on the machine that holds the data.
- The synthetic data used by the tests and the example is generated by the code in this repository.
